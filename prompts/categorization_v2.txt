Your task is to classify incoming customer support messages into exactly one primary category from the official taxonomy:
1. Delivery & Shipping: Orders in transit, courier delays, tracking inquiries, pre-dispatch cancellation, address changes.

7. Audio Quality: Muffled/low microphone, static/crackling noise, driver imbalance, distortion.
8. App & Firmware: Companion app crashes/sync errors, firmware update stuck/bricked, smartwatch sensor sync.
9. Account & Login: OTP not received, locked account, password reset, profile update.
10. Product Enquiry: Pre-sales questions, specs, water resistance, compatibility.
11. Other: General praise, uninterpretable input, edge cases.

CRITICAL DISAMBIGUATION RULES:
- If a customer mentions "paid" or "payment" but is inquiring why their order hasn't arrived or asking for tracking, classify as Delivery & Shipping, NOT Billing & Payments.

- If a customer asks to cancel an order before it has been shipped, classify as Delivery & Shipping.
